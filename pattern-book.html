The Table Pattern Book

One finish per job. When the same kind of part appears on two screens it is built the same way: same material, glow, depth, motion and sound. What it says and where it sits stay free. New UI takes its parts from this book; a genuinely new part is signed off and added here first.

CRT screens

Any screen that shows information which changes while the machine runs. Rebuilt as one component and signed off in the CRT Lab: dark glass · glow 1 · scanlines 4 · grain 4 · flicker 4 · rolling bar 1 · VHS tears 4 · ghosting 2 · channel change · mono ink.

The frame's band and gap are fixed; narrow phones only move it closer to the screen edge..dash-frame (#your-seat-dock), .dash-frame-base (#action-area)
BayHard sunk: near-black, 2px ink edge, a hard 5px shadow along the top, a lit lip below..dash-bay
Rim lightChannel, bold: a groove round the frame, dark at rest. The machine's one state light: your turn amber (steady), all in red (slow pulse), a win warm (brief), bust flickers out and stays dark. Every switch-on steps in with a relay click. The cards always stand in front of it; the win shake moves the whole machine, never the case alone.data-rim on .dash-frame, set by js/dashboard.js
Reduced MotionNo relay flicker, pulse or shake; the win is a steady warm light and bust is simply dark.
