P.I.P.
Your coach. Switch him on with the P.I.P. key next to ⚙ on the table. He stays quiet until you tap his screen, then reads the table as it is right then: tap again to go deeper. When his light blinks amber, he has something to say about the hand you just played.

Dealing

Deal styles
Switch on the ways you'd like cards dealt. Common and uncommon styles share the hands; rare (about 1 hand in 50), epic (1 in 80) and legendary (1 in 300) ones are a surprise. Once per hand, the style deals every card of the hand; every card, each card picks its own.
