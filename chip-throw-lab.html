BY BET: a call is a low flick, a raise a lob of handfuls, a big raise a shove along the felt, an all-in a heave (handfuls, then a splash). COIN TOSS: one or two coins per throw go high, flipping, with a glint. JUICE: dust puffs, chute rattle, glints, and a hit-stop and shake on all-ins.
