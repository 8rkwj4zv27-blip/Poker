MORE (default): a call is one or two coins, a raise of 400 about a dozen, an all-in a proper splash of 16 to 24. The pot holds about 60 coins and a bet only throws what the pot still has room for, so every pile on the felt sweeps in whole and nothing melts away; once the pot is full a bet throws a single coin and the numbers carry the rest. Your bank holds up to 40; a big win pours the extra into the hatch. FEW and SOME throw less; LOTS is v9. PLAY HAND deals a real hand: the bar turns into FOLD / CALL / RAISE / ALL-IN when it's your move. BOARD 3·4·5 lays out a flop, turn or river. Tap the felt to tidy; tap a tower to topple it.
