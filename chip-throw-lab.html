STACK STYLE (with SNAP): LOOSE is v4's slight wobble, STRAIGHT dead-centre towers, LEAN every stack leaning one way a pixel per coin. TIDY SHAPE is what a tap (or MESS→TIDY) squares the pile into: SPREAD short stacks in two rows, TOWERS a single row of tall stacks. SOUND: CLACK a hard block click, CLACK+ with a heavier thud, CLAY a real poker-chip tick, THOCK a deep soft thock, THUD heavy and low, COIN a gold ring, CLINK bright metal, RETRO an 8-bit blip, POP a cartoon pop, OLD the game's current chips. POT TRAY: WELL a shallow dish pressed into the felt, PANEL a printed box, BRASS a raised tray; with TRAY LIP on, coins sliding in the pot bounce off its rim (fast ones hop over). BET NUMBERS: POP-UP shows a seat's number only while its coins land. Tap a tower on the felt to topple it; tap a loose coin to flick it. RANDOM SAME makes replays identical, for comparing. Tap the bank to tidy it; with MESS·TAP, tap the felt.
