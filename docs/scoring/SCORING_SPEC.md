## 1. What the counter is

The canonical description. Used verbatim wherever the counter is explained —
the Scoring Guide, this file, and any future copy:

> **An ephemeral machine tally generated by positive chip results and objective
> milestones. It is not profit, skill, XP or Career progression.**

Two properties are accepted and recorded here so they are never later
"discovered" as defects:

- **Its units are not money or chips.** They are converted chip results plus flat
  milestone bonuses. It must never carry a `$` and must never be described as an
  amount paid out.
- **It rewards churn, variance and lucky high-risk play.** `POT WINNINGS` is
  floored at zero, so losing hands cost nothing and the tally is a monotonic
  function of gross chips won on winning hands. A loose player who wins big and
  loses big will out-score a disciplined one over the same run. The mitigation is
  that the tally is display-only, resets per Career event, and gates nothing —
  **not** that the incentive is absent.

Career Event Score is the same tally with a per-event reset. It is display-only:
it grants no access, no XP, no perks, no currency and no poker advantage, and it
is never persisted.

## 2. The authoritative award table

Every scoring award. Anything not in this table does not score.

| Award | Points | Exact trigger | Presentation timing | Persistence | Modes |
|---|---:|---|---|---|---|
| `POT WINNINGS` | `round(netProfit / bigBlind × 25 × tableMultiplier / 5) × 5`, floored at 0 | `netProfit = human.chips − g._humanStart`, read after the payout is credited | first line of the reward breakdown, before the pot smash | Single Player: run score → `felt.arcade.highScore`. | `BIG WIN` | 150 | `netProfit > 0` **and** `netProfit / bigBlind ≥ BIG_WIN_BB` | reward breakdown; escalates the smash tier to `strong` | as above | Both |
| `MASSIVE WIN` | 500 | `netProfit > 0` **and** `netProfit / bigBlind ≥ MASSIVE_WIN_BB` | reward breakdown; escalates the smash tier to `jackpot` | as above | Both |
| `DOUBLE UP` | 300 | `human.chips ≥ startOfHand × 1.9` **and** `gain ≥ 10 × bigBlind` | reward breakdown | as above | Both |
| `MONSTER HAND` | 200 | best hand is a full house or better, **revealed at showdown**, **and** `netProfit > 0` | reward breakdown | as above | Both |

| `TABLE CLEARED` | 1000 | the last opponent is eliminated and the human survives | folded into the `TABLE CLEARED` result stage; never a separate carousel | as above | **Single Player only** |

`BIG_WIN_BB = 12` and `MASSIVE_WIN_BB = 30` are **frozen** (section 9, decision 2).
They were originally tuned against the whole-table contested pot, a quantity 2–5×
larger than net profit, and were re-derived against the fixture matrix on net
profit at Phase 4A before being fixed. They live in `js/04-modes-and-scoring.js`
as `ARCADE_BIG_WIN_BB` / `ARCADE_MASSIVE_WIN_BB`, and
`validation/scoring-checks.js` pins them to the fixture module's own copy so the
two can never drift apart.

### 2.1 Why net profit, and not a pot measurement

Three definitions were compared against five worked cases (the arithmetic is in
`validation/fixtures/scoring-fixtures-cases.js`, fixtures F7 and F9–F22):

| | S1 side-pot loss | S2 genuine fold-win | S3 exact chop | S4 huge gross, tiny net | S5 genuine big win |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| whole-table contested pot (**current**) | 110bb → fires ✗ | 0 → silent ✗ | 40bb → fires ✗ | fires ✗ | fires ✓ |
| gross award minus uncalled returns, plus a net gate | ✓ | inflated | needs the second gate | needs a high second gate | inflated |
| **net profit in big blinds** ✅ | −35bb → silent ✓ | 20bb → fires ✓ | 0 → silent ✓ | 6bb → silent ✓ | 25bb → fires ✓ |

Net profit is the only definition correct in all five with a single knob. It needs
no change to `computePots()` or `handleFoldWin()`, and it cannot count the
player's own returned money because it is a difference of two stack readings.

**Recorded objection.** `POT WINNINGS` is already linear in the same quantity, so
`BIG WIN` is a step function on an axis the score already tracks. It is retained
because its real job is to escalate the presentation tier. **Settled** by section
9, decision 2: the flat point value is kept alongside the tier escalation.

### 2.2 What does not score

Every decision award — `GOOD/GREAT/HUGE FOLD`, `GOOD/GREAT CALL`, `HERO CALL`,
`GOOD/GREAT/MONSTER BLUFF`, `GOOD PRESSURE`, `GOOD/MAX VALUE`, `TRAP WORKED`,
`PUNISH`, `GOOD/GREAT SHOVE` — is removed from scoring **and** from presentation.
Luck tags and the negative catalogue remain scoreless and never subtract.

## 3. Commentary

### 3.1 Why decision-quality verdicts are deferred entirely

The evaluator's opponent model is `estimateEquity()`, which deals opponents
**uniformly random hole cards** with no action conditioning
(`js/01-poker-math.js`). A player facing a pot-sized river shove is not facing a
random hand, and the evaluator has no representation of that fact.

Widening a margin only reduces sampling noise. It does not correct the modelling
error. The current evaluator therefore **cannot** confidently declare
`RIGHT PRICE`, `GOOD CALL`, `BAD CALL`, `GOOD FOLD`, `BAD FOLD`,
`VALUE LEFT BEHIND`, `GOOD SHOVE`, `BAD SHOVE`, or any equivalent verdict — at
any threshold.

**No decision-commentary infrastructure is built.** Not behind a flag, not
disabled, not scaffolded. A permanently-disabled feature is code and tests to
maintain for no shipping behaviour. What is preserved is only what a shipping
feature needs: `captureArcadeDecision()`'s snapshots stay, because
`classifyArcadeLuck()` reads their sizing and commitment fields. Nothing that
ships reads `snap.equity`, so the capture stops requesting decision-time equity,
which also removes the invalid model's per-decision worker traffic from the live
game. The in-hand `#coach` instrument makes its own independent
`EquityService.get(…, 600)` call and is unaffected.

Detection, calibration, copy and tests for decision analysis belong to a separate,
separately approved workstream — *action-conditioned range model and decision
analysis*. It is not scheduled by this file.

### 3.2 The hidden-information rule

**Cards that were not shown to the player may not affect commentary in any way** —
not its wording, not its eligibility, not which candidate wins priority, and
**not whether a message appears at all**. The absence of a message is itself
information.

Cards may influence commentary only when they were genuinely revealed at
showdown, or are the player's own.

| Trigger | Violation | Disposition |
|---|---|---|
| `MONSTER BLUFF`, `GREAT BLUFF`, `GOOD BLUFF` | fire only when a **folded** opponent held a better or two-pair-plus hand — never shown | **Remove.** No range model fixes a hidden-card dependency. |
| `HERO CALL` | retrospective confirmation against `lastAggressorId`, who may have mucked | **Remove.** |
| `PUNISH` | reads the AI's hidden `personality.tightness` | **Remove.** |
| `LUCKY` / `VERY LUCKY` / `FILTHY` / `UNLUCKY` / `BRUTAL` | `resolvedEquityAtSnapshot()` reads every opponent's `p.hand`; public at a showdown, **hidden on a fold-win** | **Keep, gated to showdown-revealed opponents only.** |
| `confirmedGoodRead` suppression | hidden cards decide whether a message appears | **Remove the suppression.** |
| `foldSnapshotNote()` on a river fold-win | describes the winner's never-shown hand | **Fix**: restrict to showdown winners, or to the board alone. |

A public fold-win may carry neutral wording — *the pot came without a showdown* —
which claims nothing about what was folded.

### 3.3 Version 1 catalogue

| Category | Contents | Ships |
|---|---|---|
| **A — objective and safe** | settlement facts, public betting actions, the player's own cards, the board | **yes** |
| **B — requires shown cards** | an opponent's holding, only when revealed at showdown | **yes, showdown-gated** |
| **C — requires a future range model** | every decision-quality verdict | **no — not built** |
| **D — remove** | hidden-card-dependent and personality-reading triggers | **no** |

**Category A**

**Detection refined at Phase 4D.** `UNCALLED BET RETURNED` is detected as
*the portion won from single-contributor layers exceeds the hand's net profit* —
"more of what came back was your own stake than you actually gained" — rather
than as the stricter "won only layers with exactly 1 contributor" below. The
strict form does not fire on fixture F22, an approved case that requires it: a
1,980 uncalled shove returned behind $50 of matched blinds nets +1.5bb, and the
player won the small matched layers too. The refined form separates F22 from
fixture F7, a genuine +20bb fold-win carrying a small unmatched remainder. It
still rests entirely on `computePots()`'s contributor count, exactly as below.

| Situation | Detection (all public) | Illustrative line |
|---|---|---|
| Won a **contested** layer while finishing behind | won ≥1 layer with 2+ **contributors** and `netProfit < 0` | `SIDE POT WON · NET LOSS` |
| Only an **unmatched contribution** came back | won only layers with exactly 1 contributor | `UNCALLED BET RETURNED` |
| Exact split | a layer with `split === true` including `'you'`, `netProfit ≈ 0` | `POT CHOPPED · STAKE RETURNED` |
| Pressure succeeded | fold-win the human won, own aggression above threshold | `NO SHOWDOWN · POT TAKEN` |
| Large genuine win | `netProfit ≥ BIG_WIN_BB` | *carried by the `BIG WIN` award* |
| Knockout / double-up | `koCount > 0` / `chips ≥ 1.9 × start` | *carried by the awards* |
| Bust / terminal victory | terminal conditions | *carried by the result stage; no line* |

The first two rows are **different facts and must never share wording.** Winning a
$40 contested side pot is opponents' money genuinely won; an uncalled bet coming
back is the player's own stake. Telling them apart requires the contributor count
that `computePots()` currently computes and discards — see §7.4 — so
`computePots()` gains one additive field, `contributors: payers.length`, alongside
the unchanged `eligible`. That is a commentary-driven change; the scoring
definition in §2 needs no such field.

**Category B**

| Situation | Gate | Illustrative line |
|---|---|---|
| Bad beat / fortunate escape | showdown **and** every opponent used by `resolvedEquityAtSnapshot()` was revealed | existing `BRUTAL` / `UNLUCKY` / `LUCKY` / `VERY LUCKY` / `FILTHY` |
| Monster hand shown | full house or better, revealed | *carried by the `MONSTER HAND` award* |
| Own bluff shown down | the **human's own** revealed holding was weak after significant aggression | `SHOWN DOWN LIGHT` |

**Category C — not built:** `GOOD/GREAT/HUGE FOLD`, `GOOD/GREAT CALL`,
`GOOD/MAX VALUE`, `TRAP WORKED`, `GOOD/GREAT SHOVE`, `GOOD PRESSURE` as a quality
claim, `LOOSE CALL`, `BAD CALL`, `PAID THEM OFF`, `BAD FOLD`, `TOO TIGHT`,
`BAD BLUFF`, `RECKLESS`, `BAD SHOVE`, `OVERPLAYED`, `MISSED VALUE`, `TOO PASSIVE`.

**Category D — removed:** `MONSTER BLUFF`, `GREAT BLUFF`, `GOOD BLUFF`,
`HERO CALL`, `PUNISH`.

Illustrative lines are examples, not approved final copy.

### 3.4 Priority and cadence

- **At most one commentary line per hand**, in addition to whatever the award or
  result layer already says. Never two.
- Priority, first match wins:
  1. terminal condition → **nothing**;
  2. contested win at a net loss / exact split / uncalled bet returned;
  3. showdown luck tag;
  4. own bluff shown down;
  5. pressure succeeded.
- **Rate limit:** the soft lines (4, 5) fire at most once every **N hands**,
  working default **N = 4**. The hard settlement facts (2, 3) are exempt — they
  are rare by construction and always worth stating.

**Clarification added at Phase 4D.** Priority 1 governs **delivery**, never
detection — section 4's rule that the terminal condition suppresses celebration
but not detection, mutation or persistence applies here too, so a terminal hand
still evaluates its line and still records a public luck tag in the profile.

The fixture matrix forced one further distinction into the open, because two
approved fixtures are both terminal winning hands and disagree about whether a
line appears:

| Case | Delivered | Why |
|---|---|---|
| terminal **busting** hand | **nothing at all** | straight to `RUN OVER` / `EVENT LOST`, without exception (F12) |
| terminal **winning** hand, settlement or soft line | **nothing** | the result stage is about to state the settlement itself; a line narrating it first both duplicates and pre-empts it (F19, an uncalled return on the table-clearing hand) |
| terminal **winning** hand, showdown luck tag | **delivered** | it says something the result stage never says, about cards the player genuinely saw (F3, a 0%-equity suck-out that cleared the table) |
| ordinary hand | whatever won the ladder | — |

The rule lives in one function, `arcadeCommentaryText()`, which is also what the
validation harnesses ask rather than restating it.

### 3.5 Delivery surface

| Surface | Role |
|---|---|
| **`#banner`** — the CRT action line (`role="status"`, `aria-live="polite"`) | **the default commentary surface.** One short line at the existing `Hand complete.` beat |
| **`#review`** — the optional Hand Review panel (`settings.review`) | **expanded** analysis. Never the only place an observation appears |
| `#arcade-reward-layer` | scoring awards only. **Commentary never uses it** |
| result stages | terminal outcomes only |

Terminal suppression is structurally free on the CRT line: the terminal branches
of `finishHand()` return before the `Hand complete.` beat, and the result path
sets its own banner.

All message text comes from one lookup, `commentaryLine(id, context)`, where
`context` carries only public quantities. Detection, eligibility, priority and
cadence never consult the text source, so a future Coach character can supply
different wording for the same `id` without touching any of them. **The seam is
designed; the Coach is not built.**

### 3.6 No solver certainty

No "you should have", no "the correct play was", no percentages, no EV figures,
no counterfactual results. Describe the price, the situation and the settlement;
never grade the person. Every line must remain true if the player's read was
better than the machine's.

## 4. Terminal hands: the four responsibilities, separated

A terminal hand may still legitimately earn objective score. The rule suppresses
contradictory **celebration**, never detection, mutation or persistence.

| | Terminal **winning** hand | Terminal **busting** hand |
|---|---|---|
| **Detection** | full objective evaluator runs, including `koCount` and the terminal award | full objective evaluator runs; structurally finds nothing, but whatever it finds is honoured |
| **Score mutation** | every earned point added once; `displayedScore` set equal without a roll | identical |
| **Presentation** | the **early** pot-smash for the hand's own payout is retained. The **late** carousel is **suppressed**; its points are revealed once, on the result stage | **nothing at all** — straight to `RUN OVER` / `EVENT LOST` |
| **Persistence** | `finalizeArcadeRun()` / stage-model construction runs **after** mutation | identical |

Implementation shape: `resolveArcadeHandLate()` splits into
`resolveArcadeHandScore()` (detect and mutate, silent) and the existing
`presentArcadeResolution()`. `finishHand()` evaluates the terminal conditions
**before** deciding whether to present.

## 6. Fixture determinism contract

Two random paths exist in the evaluator. Both are closed, and the audit harness
enforces the closure:

1. **`snap.equityPromise` → `estimateEquity()`** — unseeded Monte Carlo.
   **Closed by injection:** every fixture snapshot carries an explicit numeric
   `equity`, and `settleArcadeSnapshots()` awaits the promise only when
   `s.equity == null`.
2. **`classifyArcadeLuck()` → `resolvedEquityAtSnapshot()`** — samples 520 random
   runouts when three or more board cards are unknown. **Closed by construction:**
   every snapshot whose action is not `fold` or `check` is taken with a board of at
   least three cards, so `need ≤ 2` and the function enumerates exhaustively.

Injecting `snap.equity` alone is **not** sufficient — it does not reach path 2.
The board-length rule is a hard property of every fixture and
`validation/scoring-audit.js` exits non-zero if any fixture violates it. A fixture
that genuinely cannot satisfy it must declare `luckStubbed: true` and supply
`resolvedLuckEquity`, and the harness substitutes a deterministic function in its
own scope — no production seam. **No fixture currently needs the stub.**

**Harness exit codes.** `validation/scoring-audit.js` exits **0** when it ran
cleanly and every fixture produced a comparable result, *including* when
divergences were found — divergences are the report's output, not a failure. It
exits **non-zero** on a broken harness, a missing fixture id, a malformed fixture,
or any error thrown while evaluating a fixture.

Phase 4 adds `validation/scoring-checks.js`, which consumes the same fixture data
and asserts the `expected` blocks for real, landing gate by gate alongside the
corrections that make it pass. No committed check ever fails, and no passing
assertion preserves a defect.

### 7.1 The two reported failures, reproduced

**Failure A — celebration before defeat (fixture F12).** A correct-price call
loses, the player busts. The evaluator awards `GOOD CALL` **+100**, the late
carousel presents it full-screen with sound and a score roll, and only then does
`RUN OVER` appear. `resolveArcadeHandLate()` is awaited in `finishHand()` *before*
the `human.chips <= 0` check.

Attribution — four separate faults, which must not be conflated:

- **detection** — the verdict rests on equity against uniformly random hands;
- **scoring** — points are granted on a hand the player lost;
- **sequencing** — presentation is awaited between the bust and the defeat stage;
- **wording** — `GOOD CALL` with `+100` on a reward reveal is celebration.

The correct-but-lost *analysis* is not itself wrong. A good decision can lose.
What is wrong is that it scores, how it is worded, and when it appears.

**Failure B — `BIG POT` on a net-losing hand (fixture F9).** Quads nines wins a
$40 side pot while quads kings takes the $2,160 main pot; the player is **−$700**
on the hand. Measured current behaviour:

- `MASSIVE POT` **+500** — `arcadeContestedPot()` sums the whole table's $2,200;
- `MONSTER HAND` **+200** — gated only on having won *a* pot layer;
- `GOOD CALL` **+100**;
- `luck: FILTHY` — the player "won" a pot with 0% equity;
- the **full reward breakdown and pot smash ceremony** run.

**800 points and a victory ceremony for a $700 loss.** The reported symptom
understated it: three awards fire, not one.

### 7.3 Score mutation is coupled to presentation

Neither path mutates the score outside its own presentation:
`presentArcadeAward()` moves `a.score` on the late path, and
`runPotSmashSequence()`'s `rollScore()` moves it on the early path. **Suppressing
presentation would therefore also suppress the score.** This is precisely why §4
separates the four responsibilities rather than adding an early return.

### 7.4 `computePots()` discards the number the commentary needs

`computePots()` computes `payers.length` per layer and keeps only `eligible`
(payers who did not fold). Measured on F22, three layers — $30 paid into by 3
players, $20 by 2, $1,980 by 1 — **all expose `eligible = 1`** and are
indistinguishable through what the function returns. This is the root of both the
over-firing (D7's mirror) and the inability to separate `SIDE POT WON` from
`UNCALLED BET RETURNED`.

## 8. Persistence disclosure

**Career persists no score anywhere.** Measured (F14/F16): a full Career
evaluation leaves `felt.arcade` byte-identical. Event Score is genuinely
ephemeral and display-only, exactly as `CAREER_DESIGN.md` claims.

**Single Player's permanent profile is contaminated, and cannot be repaired.**
`noteArcadeDiscovery()` writes unconditionally for every award the evaluator
produces, and `finalizeArcadeRun()` writes the run total to
`felt.arcade.highScore` **after** the bust hand's own awards have been banked.
Measured on F15: a `GOOD CALL` awarded on the very hand that ended the run reaches
`felt.arcade.highScore` permanently.

Running this fixture set alone leaves counts for `massivePot`, `goodCall`,
`goodPressure`, `greatShove`, `monsterHand` and others — awards this
specification removes or redefines.

**Decision: leave it, and disclose it.** The affected values are
`felt.stats.won`, `felt.stats.showdownsWon`, `felt.stats.biggestPot`, and
`felt.arcade`'s `highScore`, `counts`, `discovered` and `bestByEvent`. They cannot
be recomputed — the hands are gone — and resetting them would destroy legitimate
history alongside the contamination. The project rule is to preserve lifetime
statistics unless a task requires otherwise, and nothing here requires it.
**No stored history is changed by Phase 4.**

## 9. Settled owner decisions

All six were **settled and approved by the owner on 2026-08-26**, each on the
recommendation carried in the approved plan. They are no longer open, and
Phase 4 implements them as written below.

| # | Decision | Settled as |
|---:|---|---|
| 1 | **Career Event Score** | **Kept.** An ephemeral, display-only, per-event machine tally under §1's description, with the churn incentive recorded rather than mitigated. It is not XP, not progression and not currency, and it gates nothing. |
| 2 | **`BIG WIN` semantics** | **Renamed and redefined on net profit**, retaining *both* the flat points and the presentation-tier escalation. `MONSTER HAND` continues to score, under its two gates (full house or better, **revealed at showdown**, and `netProfit > 0`). |
| 3 | **Hands won / tied / partially returned** | **§5.1 adopted.** A `ties` counter is deferred; `DEFAULT_STATS` keeps its current shape. Recording is corrected going forward only. |
| 4 | **Default commentary surface** | **The `#banner` CRT line**, with the Hand Review panel as expanded analysis and never the only place an observation appears. |
| 5 | **Decision-quality commentary** | **Deferred entirely** to the separate action-conditioned range-model workstream. The accepted cost is that `RIGHT PRICE · TOUGH RESULT` does not ship in v1. No decision-commentary infrastructure is built — not behind a flag, not disabled, not scaffolded. |
| 6 | **Character lost to §3.2** | **Removal accepted.** `MONSTER BLUFF`, `GREAT BLUFF`, `GOOD BLUFF`, `HERO CALL` and `PUNISH` are removed from scoring and from presentation, replaced by one neutral public line where a public fact remains to state. |

Decision 2 also freezes the two thresholds. `BIG_WIN_BB = 12` and
`MASSIVE_WIN_BB = 30` were re-derived against the fixture matrix at Phase 4A
rather than carried forward unexamined, and the matrix confirms them: on net
profit they leave F19 (+6bb on a 56bb gross collection) and F22 (+1.5bb behind a
1,980 uncalled return) **silent**, fire `BIG WIN` on F7, F10, F13, F14 and F20
(20–25bb of genuine profit), and reserve `MASSIVE WIN` for F1 (+40bb). They are
no longer provisional.

## 10. What shipped, gate by gate — 2026-08-26

Phase 4 ran as four sequential gates. All three validation suites were run after
each; none was allowed to proceed on a failure.

### 4A — pot attribution and lifetime statistics

- `arcadeContestedPot()` **deleted** and replaced by `arcadeNetProfit()`. Every
  win-size award is now measured against the player's net result for the hand,
  not the whole table's matched money (D7 and its mirror).
- `BIG POT` / `MASSIVE POT` renamed to `BIG WIN` / `MASSIVE WIN`, sharing one
  `winSize` family, gated on `netProfit > 0` and the frozen bb thresholds.
  `MONSTER HAND` and `DOUBLE UP` fall under the same net-positive gate.
- `HERO CALL` (D8) and `PUNISH` (D12) removed, with `findCallerOf()`.
- **D5:** K.O. attribution made mode-blind in `resolveEliminations()`. The run
  counters stay gated on `g.run`; only the per-hand count returned to
  `finishHand()` is shared, so Career fires K.O. and writes no run state.
- **D6:** `EVENT WON` added; Career awards it where Single Player awards
  `TABLE CLEARED`. Same condition, same value, two names.
- **D13 / D14:** `recordPot()` deleted. One writer, `recordHandStatistics()`,
  called once per hand from `finishHand()` after the payout, records
  `won`, `showdownsWon` and `biggestPot` from the settled net result.
  `trackEliminationHand()` takes the same quantity, so `run.biggestPotWon` and
  `tableBiggestPotWon` record net profit rather than the gross share.
- The three §5.2 label changes and the two `career-result-checks.js`
  assertions that pin them, in this gate.
- `validation/scoring-checks.js` created. The VM harness both scoring files
  share was extracted to `validation/fixtures/scoring-harness.js`, so the report
  and the test suite cannot drive the evaluator differently.

### 4B — decision awards removed from scoring and presentation

- The entire decision catalogue and `evaluateArcadeSkill()`,
  `findArcadeFoldBluff()`, `isMonsterBluff()`, `heroCatAtSnapshot()` and
  `actionWasCalled()` **deleted**, not disabled. `ARCADE_NEGATIVE` and
  `evaluateArcadeNegative()` deleted with them, which also removes D11.
- `captureArcadeDecision()` no longer requests decision-time equity. The
  snapshots remain, because `classifyArcadeLuck()` reads their sizing.
- The Scoring Guide, the awards glossary, the DEV panel and the DEV pot-smash
  fixtures follow the objective catalogue. The guide now opens with §1's
  canonical description of the counter, verbatim.

### 4C — terminal-hand calculation and presentation ordering

- `resolveArcadeHandLate()` split into `resolveArcadeHandScore()` (detect and
  record, silent) and either `bankArcadeResolution()` (terminal: add the points,
  set `displayedScore` equal, show nothing) or the unchanged
  `presentArcadeResolution()`.
- `finishHand()` computes `terminal` **before** the late pass and passes it in.
- `evaluateArcadeAwardsEarly()` returns `null` when nothing was earned, so a
  hand won on a layer but lost overall pays its chips through the ordinary
  payout path with no reward layer, TOTAL or pot smash.

### 4D — objective commentary and the hidden-information rule

- `computePots()` gained the additive `contributors` field (§7.4). Showdown pot
  rows carry it; a fold-win reads the layering directly, since its winner takes
  every layer.
- `classifyArcadeLuck()` gated by `luckOpponentsWereRevealed()`: every opponent
  the calculation would use must be a revealed showdown contender (D10).
- `foldSnapshotNote()` takes an explicit `shown` argument and is no longer
  called on a fold-win (D9). The fold-win review states the public fact instead.
- `ARCADE_COMMENTARY`, one text lookup `commentaryLine(id, context)`, the
  settlement detector `arcadeSettlementFacts()`, the priority ladder
  `evaluateArcadeCommentary()`, and the `N = 4` cadence limiter on the soft
  lines, whose counter lives on the mode's own reward state.
- Delivery moved to the `#banner` CRT line at the `Hand complete.` beat.
  `presentArcadeCommentary()` deleted — the reward layer is scoring awards only.
