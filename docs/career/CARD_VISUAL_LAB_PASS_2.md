# Career Card Visual Lab — Pass 2

Owner-directed isolated visual exploration, 9 October 2026. Review surface:
`career-card-visual-lab.html`. No production integration is approved by this lab.

## Preview

Serve the repository root and open `/career-card-visual-lab.html`. The review
session serves it at `http://127.0.0.1:8766/career-card-visual-lab.html`.

Tap any card to lift it into inspection. Hold and move to tilt it; release to
settle. CATCH THE LIGHT replays the card finish; the Common Table Event instead
offers LIFT THE STOCK. Arrow keys or the two physical arrow keys cycle the set.
CLOSE, Escape or a backdrop tap returns the card and keyboard focus to its tray.
The machine's MOTION switch is temporary; the device's Reduced Motion preference
always takes precedence.

## Construction

The production foundation supplies both embedded pixel fonts and existing
velvet, cream, felt, ink and button-material tokens. The lab uses its own
`ccl-` classes, stylesheet and script. The cabinet borrows the Hub's physical
housing, static recessed instrument and ticket-print hierarchy without loading
the Hub's state or entry code.

All four cards share a renderer: stepped stock silhouette, header, title, art
panel, type line, rarity band and numbered footer. Illustrations are authored
filled SVG geometry on a 96 × 72 grid. No generated images, network assets,
photographic textures, framework or dependencies are involved.

| Card | Material / print | Inspect finish |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Table Event · Common | Cream house stock, forest ink, modest single keyline | Clean lift and hard separated shadow |
| Freezeout · Rare | Pale icy stock, silver-blue double trim, richer header | Narrow cool sheen |
| Bomb · Common | Warm stock, danger band, authored pixel scorch corners | Five small cinders and restrained warmth |
| Wild Event · Legendary | Dark plum stock, segmented prismatic frame and foil band | Foil sweep, three glints, finger-responsive sheen |

Rarity remains printed and readable when effects are off. Inspect effects are
finite and live on the art surface; the drawer stays still. Moving a held card
is bounded, and it returns to neutral on release, cancellation or window blur.

## Boundaries and compromises

- These are display specimens, not playable events, owned inventory or rewards.
  Bomb and Wild do not imply new poker rules, currencies or approved Career
  catalogue additions. No fabricated entry prices or payout terms are shown.
- No production JavaScript, storage, save, settings, statistics, transaction,
  service-worker registration, manifest or live navigation is accessed.
- The supplied attachment contained the written brief only. No card reference
  images were available; the four written identities guided the artwork.
- Short phones scroll the complete drawer. Desktop retains a phone-shaped
  cabinet. The finish is a restrained CSS approximation of printed foil.
- Actual iPhone Safari/PWA feel remains an owner review item; this lab is not
  included in the production PWA cache.
