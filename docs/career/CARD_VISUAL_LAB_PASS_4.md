## Review surface

`career-card-visual-lab.html?pass=4` opens in **Compare Rarities** mode.
Choose TABLE, ICE, BOMB or WILD to see that event in reading order C / UC / R / L.
**Event Drawer** shows all four event types at one rarity, selected using
C / UC / R / L. Tap any card to inspect; arrows traverse the visible four.
The selected comparison key retains keyboard focus after a specimen change.

## Material progression

- Common: restrained forest-green keyline, ordinary print, event-specific stock.
- Uncommon: mint double rim, lacquered art frame, selective reflection and
  authored event-specific pixel accents.
- Rare: thicker blue/silver rim, reverse-printed header, metallic art corners,
  engraved prize texture and restrained spot gloss.
- Legendary: prismatic rim/art frame, spectral title/prize ink, premium stock
  and full-card responsive foil. Dark pigments on light stock preserve contrast;
  Wild keeps pale reflective ink on its dark stock.

Burn damage and external embers belong to Bomb at every rarity. Freezeout
retains icy stock and frozen chips. Table remains a cream poker invitation;
Wild remains a strange purple house invitation even without Legendary foil.
Rarity, event material, artwork and surface finish are separate data/style axes.
Foil responds to bounded pointer/touch movement in one update per display frame;
opening/replay effects stop. System and temporary lab Reduced Motion remain.
