# Career Card Visual Lab — Pass 6: Collector editions

Owner-directed work, 2026-10-10. The owner approved Pass 5's Table progression
and authorised extending that direction to Bomb, Wild and Freezeout, with the
same critical review and refinement. This is an isolated presentation milestone.
The approved Table artwork, materials and drawer rendering are preserved.

## Built

All four event families now have four authored editions. Common artwork stays
intact. The three new premium scenes per family change composition, print
coverage and physical treatment rather than recolouring a single illustration.
The cabinet, card dimensions, four-card drawer and inspection routes remain.

- Bomb: orange scorched invitation; charcoal/copper warning print; oxblood and
  silver machinery with a fractured foreground casing; blackened copper
  Legendary dominated by an unstable bomb. Legendary adds genuinely missing
  chunks on both sides, traced hot damage, warm foil ink and larger embers.
- Wild: restrained house invitation; purple colour impression; an impossible
  nested-card picture with foreground cards escaping the silver frame; full-face
  Legendary with one dominant crooked grin, a twisting card fan and faint
  displaced faces. Rightward inspection tilt gives the main face a wink.
- Freezeout: pale cold-pressed invitation; teal glacier print; enlarged frozen
  stacks and fractured foreground ice over silver stock; midnight Legendary
  with monumental ice formations, poker cards and cold reflective print.

Rarity markers and rims remain independent of event identity. Uncommon keeps a
cream prize stub, Rare a silver plate, and Legendary a quiet dark plaque.
Legendary patterned foil covers the face, title, art, prize and rim, while
individual patterns/palettes distinguish copper, spectral house print and ice.
The default comparison opens Bomb; Table is labelled as the approved set.

## Critical assessment and corrections

Reviewed at actual drawer card sizes, enlarged inspection, motion off and with
rarity letters/edition labels hidden. Additional grayscale evidence hides the
prize amounts so typography, money and colour cannot carry the progression.

The first Bomb Legendary placed its fuse across the title. Its cracks also
obscured too much of the expression. The fuse was rerouted into the illustration,
and fracture paths moved around the eyes. Header spacing was corrected around
burnt corners. Legendary side ruptures add a visible physical upgrade at rest.

The first Wild Legendary relied too heavily on enlargement. Its expression now
has an asymmetric brow and broader grin, with secondary ghost impressions kept
faint so one face remains dominant. The wink is a discovery during inspection;
the static composition carries the rarity without it.

Freezeout intentionally stays calmer than Bomb/Wild. Expanded foreground ice
makes Rare substantially different from the modest Common picture. Legendary
uses a full-face midnight scene with tall facets and a single central frozen
stack, preserving cold contrast and readable prize print.

In all families the progression remains visible in grayscale and at rest:
ordinary framed invitation, bold colour print with cream stub, expanded Rare
scene/foreground depth, then exclusive full-face Legendary. Common remains a
pleasing ordinary collectible. Agent assessment: ready for owner creative review
against the approved Table direction. Owner approval for these new families is
pending; actual iPhone feel and 120Hz performance are not established by browser
emulation.
